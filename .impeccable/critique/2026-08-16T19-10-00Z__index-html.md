## Priority Issues (new this pass)

[P1] Home copy promises a "newsletter" that doesn't exist. The "Follow along" section says "the site keeps it all in one place — the feed, the newsletter, and the calendar," then pushes off-site to Instagram/Facebook. No newsletter exists (Join was removed). Fix: rewrite to what exists; don't re-add a form.

[P1] Gatherings page has no <h1> (heading regression missed in the fix pass). Outline starts at H2 → H3; Home/Story/Event all have an H1. SR users get no page heading on the primary conversion route; SEO miss. Fix: make the gatherings intro an H1.

[P2] Event-detail media dwarfs the event. The 3/2 media (~600px tall desktop) pushes title/description/tags below the fold; in the no-photo fallback it's a giant teal void. Fix: cap media height (~360–420px) or move title/meta above it.

[P2] RSVP modal: unbranded + bot-gate on a free RSVP. (a) Only screen that drops the design system (cream box, separate font) — coldest surface at the warmest moment. (b) Cloudflare Turnstile hard-blocks submit even for a free RSVP; if Turnstile fails to load, a real user is stuck. Fix: restyle onto teal/rose (at least brand the title); drop Turnstile on the free path or fail open.

[P3] "Save My Seat" nav label ≠ destination (goes to list); category taxonomy mixes vibe + price and duplicates the price badge. Fix: relabel nav CTA to match destination; split price (badge) from vibe (chip).
