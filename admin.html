How this works
  1. Everyone who signed up for the newsletter is listed here, newest first, and each one is also written to your newsletter Google Sheet.
  2. Unsubscribed people stay in the list on purpose — that's the record that stops them being added back by a later import. Don't email them.
  3. People normally add themselves through the form and remove themselves through the link in your emails. When that isn't possible — a broken link, or an address you know shouldn't be here — use Unsubscribe on the row.
  4. Delete is for junk signups only. It wipes the row completely, which also drops the record that keeps them off the list, so that address could sign up again. For a real person, use Unsubscribe.
